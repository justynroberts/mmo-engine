# Beasts and monsters

Animated monsters by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), from the Ultimate Monsters bundle, CC0 1.0
(public domain), downloaded from Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Monsters-Bundle-5oyGWAmOB6):

- chicken.glb — Chicken (https://poly.pizza/m/ineV9pU5VL), the farmsteads' hens
- frog.glb — Frog (https://poly.pizza/m/37wofOCOzG), frogs by the lakes
- mushnub.glb — Mushnub (https://poly.pizza/m/LWKmS30Xxl), shroomlings in the woods
- mushroom_king.glb — Mushroom King (https://poly.pizza/m/grnFTziU8u)
- orc.glb — Orc (https://poly.pizza/m/5vO2YJsPEf), the Fomorians of the north
- yeti.glb — Yeti (https://poly.pizza/m/ceRHrn8HHE), snow trolls of the north and (tinted) hill trolls
- dragon_evolved.glb — Dragon Evolved (https://poly.pizza/m/LlwD0QNUPj), the lake wyrms
- ghost.glb — Ghost (https://poly.pizza/m/Iip30bDHmu), the restless dead
- ghost_skull.glb — Ghost Skull (https://poly.pizza/m/TX8r9WBXpe), cave wraiths

Their clips are named "CharacterArmature|Idle" and the like: walkers have Idle, Walk, Run, Punch, HitReact, Death;
small ones Idle, Walk, Bite_Front, HitRecieve, Death; flyers Flying_Idle, Fast_Flying, Headbutt, HitReact, Death.
scripts/animal.gd maps them onto what it plays.
