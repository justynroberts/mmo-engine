# Music credits

The client (`godot/scripts/sound.gd`) picks a random piece for the current mood from `<mood>.ogg` and `<mood>_N.ogg`.
All files here were converted to Ogg Vorbis (44.1 kHz stereo), loudness-normalised, trimmed of silence and given short
fades; the music itself is unchanged.
